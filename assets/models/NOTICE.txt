MyanmarTTS on-device models (assets/models/myanmartts_v*.onnx)
and preset voices (assets/voices/presets.json)
===================================================================

These ONNX files are converted from https://huggingface.co/freococo/MyanmarTTS
by tools/myanmartts_onnx/ in this repository.

myanmartts_v2_encoder.onnx, myanmartts_v1_prenet.onnx, myanmartts_v2_estimator.onnx,
myanmartts_v1_reference.onnx
  Converted from model_fp32.pt by freococo — CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Generated audio is also CC0 1.0.

assets/voices/presets.json
  Voice fingerprints (speaker embeddings) computed with the models above.
  - "original": from freococo/MyanmarTTS samples/sample_0.wav — CC0 1.0.
  - All other presets: from speech recordings in Google FLEURS (Burmese, my_mm),
    licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
    Conneau et al., "FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal
    Representations of Speech", 2022. https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
    Changes: each recording was resampled to 44.1 kHz and reduced to a
    256-number speaker embedding; no audio from FLEURS is included in the app.
    The source file of each preset is listed in its "source" field.

myanmartts_v1_vocos.onnx
  Converted from vocos.pt, the pretrained Vocos vocoder from the StableTTS1.1
  release (https://huggingface.co/KdaiP/StableTTS1.1), under the MIT License:
